## FAQs (visible on the page; answer first; no prices, no guarantees)

Use on Home (pick 4), and on each service page (pick the ones that fit):

- **Are you a new agency?** Yes. Nexlane Digital started in 2026 and we have no client projects to show yet. We say so openly, and we offer a free audit so you can judge our thinking before you pay anything.
- **Where is Nexlane Digital based?** We are based in Kaithal, Haryana, and we work with local businesses in person and with businesses across Haryana remotely.
- **How much does a website or marketing cost? / Kaithal me website banwane ka kharcha kitna hota hai?** The price depends on the pages, features and goals, so we give a quote after a free chat. We write the scope down before any work starts, so there are no surprises.
- **Will you get me #1 on Google?** No honest agency can promise a ranking. We work on the things we can control (your Google profile, site speed, page content, local signals) and tell you plainly what we see.
- **What is AEO/GEO?** AEO and GEO mean making your content easy for AI assistants and AI search results to understand and quote, in addition to normal Google ranking.
- **How long does local SEO take?** It usually takes weeks to months and depends on how many others compete for the same searches. Your free audit shows where you stand today.
- **Kaithal me website banwane ke liye mujhe kya chahiye?** Your business name, what you offer, a few clear photos and your contact details are enough to start. We guide you through the rest.
- **What do I get from the free audit?** A short written review of your website or Google profile with 3 to 5 practical suggestions, free and with no obligation.
